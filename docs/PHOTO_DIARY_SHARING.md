# Keep a photo diary private, then share a deliberate copy

Developer-maintained SealedPocket tutorial. Prepared with AI assistance from the published product documentation, 10 November 2026. Not an independent security review.

## A practical example

You want a travel diary with photographs and commentary, but do not want every entry automatically synchronized to a cloud journaling service. SealedPocket keeps its content on your iPhone or iPad. An optional VIP feature exports an individual diary entry as a multi-page PDF.

## A small, reviewable workflow

1. Start with a short diary entry and a few photographs. Add the context someone will need later, such as what the photograph shows and why it matters.
2. Review the complete entry before exporting. If it is intended for sharing, leave out private details that do not belong in the shared copy.
3. Use the diary PDF export if your purchase includes VIP. This exports an individual entry; do not assume it is an export of your entire library.
4. Open the saved PDF in a reader and inspect every page, photograph and paragraph. Check that the document contains only the material you intended to share.
5. Choose the recipient and destination deliberately. The exported PDF is outside SealedPocket's encrypted storage and is handled by the receiving app or service.

Importing a photograph does not delete its original from Photos. A vault copy and its original are separate copies. Likewise, making a PDF does not automatically remove private details: encrypted storage and redaction solve different problems.

## Local storage has a recovery trade-off

No app account and no automatic cloud synchronization also means there is no developer-hosted copy to recover for you. Keep an independent copy of irreplaceable content and keep your password safe. Do not uninstall the app as a troubleshooting experiment when it contains your only copy.

## Cost and platform

The free media/file allowance is 75 items, including recordings. VIP is an optional one-time purchase; it removes the import cap and includes diary PDF export. Available device storage still matters. Requires iOS/iPadOS 17.6 or later. Check current regional pricing and availability in the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755504480).

## 中文操作要点

先写一篇短日记并加入几张照片，确认说明文字和图片都适合分享，再使用 VIP 的单篇日记 PDF 导出。重新打开导出的 PDF，逐页检查后再发送。

导出的 PDF 不再由应用内加密空间保护；导入照片也不会自动删除系统相册中的原件。本地保存不等于云端备份，重要内容要另存副本，保管好密码。免费媒体与文件额度共 75 项，一次性 VIP 可解除导入数量限制，但仍受设备容量限制。
